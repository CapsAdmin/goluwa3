# Vehicles and seats

Vehicles live in the sandbox addon: definitions and their prefabs in `addons/sandbox/lua/vehicles.lua` (`car`, `motorbike`, `boat`, `plane`, the Vehicles tab of the spawn menu, `spawn_requests` kind `vehicle`), the behaviour in the `vehicle` component (`lua/components/vehicle.lua`). Seating is generic and lives in the game addon: the `seat` component, the `seat` animation state and the seat camera mode. Tests: `luajit glw test addons/sandbox/tests/entities/vehicles.lua`, `luajit glw test physics/wheel.lua`.

## Physics, no hacks

- The prefab root is the chassis: one dynamic body whose origin is its centre of mass (colliders are placed around it, so a low origin gives a low centre of mass). Every wheel is its own sphere body joined with `constraints.Wheel` (`goluwa/physics/constraints/wheel.lua`): the wheel slides on a sprung line along the suspension axis (`Stiffness`/`Damping` in N/m and N s/m, `LowerTranslation`/`UpperTranslation` travel limits), its axle is held to the chassis axle turned by `SetSteerAngle` about that same axis (positive turns left), and it spins freely about the axle except for `SetMotor(speed, torque)` (relative spin, positive rolls forward when the axle is `up x forward`) and `SetBrake(torque)`. Steering is real: the front wheel bodies turn, Ackermann angles per wheel.
- Wheel bodies set `SpinAxis` (their local axle) so the integrator spins them exactly about it; without it a fast wheel on a turning chassis tilts its axle the wrong way every step and the joint has to pull it back.
- Wheels are server/authority only, transient, not networked, `TraceOwner` is the vehicle and `ResolveUseTarget` maps them to it. Clients see a kinematic chassis and place the visual wheel nodes from a ray down per wheel, the replicated `Steer` and the forward speed.
- The motorbike stays up with `constraints.KeepUpright` in roll mode (`LocalRollAxis` forward, soft `Hertz`, `MaxTorque`), whose target is the lean that balances the turn it is making (`atan(v * yaw_rate / g)`), so it leans into corners and still stands when parked.
- The boat floats on the buoyancy cells of its hull colliders, `FluidDrag` (per body axis) lets it slide lengthwise and resist sideways, and its outboard pushes only while `fluid.GetDepth` says the propeller is under water, turned by the steering.
- The plane gets lift and drag per surface (wings, stabilizer, fin) from the local airflow, angle of attack, a lift slope that stalls into a flat plate, and control deflections added to the angle; thrust at the nose. Its stability comes from the tail behind the centre of mass and the high wing, nothing holds it level.
- Rollover is physical: a car rolls when its lateral grip exceeds `track / (2 * height of the centre of mass)` g, so keep the centre of mass low and the steering angle limited by speed (`GetMaxSteer`).

## Seats

- `seat` component on an entity (`Pose` = `drive`/`ride`/`pilot`, `Position` = pelvis point in the entity's space, `EyeHeight`, `ExitPosition`, `CameraDistance`/`CameraHeight`, and prefab node names for `HandLeft/Right` and `FootLeft/Right`). `OnUsed` enters on the authority; pressing use while seated leaves.
- The seat is the replicated `character_pose.Seat` (an entity var sent to the owner too). `character_movement` turns the capsule kinematic with collision off and puts the entity on the seat every tick, skips movement, and on leaving resets the mode and places the character at the first free spot from `seat:FindExit`. Reconcile is skipped while seated; weapons are not thought or posed (`weapon_holder:IsSeated`) and hidden (`HideWeapons` on the animation).
- `character_pose` enables the `seat` state every frame; its `Body` hook draws the character at the seat's render transform (so it never lags the interpolated vehicle) and the head looks relative to the seat. `source_pose` plays the Source sequence of the pose (`drive_jeep`, `drive_airboat`, ...), moves the pelvis onto the seat point and solves the legs to the foot nodes and the arms to the grip nodes (`leg_pose.SolveFoot`, `aim_pose.SolveGrip`); the eye body sits its eye on the seat and does the same with its own limbs. Grip nodes that are children of a turning node (steering wheel, handlebar, yoke) carry the hands with them.
- The seat camera (`lua/seat_camera.lua`, `Priority` above other camera modes) chases the seat at `CameraDistance`; the mouse wheel zooms in to first person, where the look is relative to the vehicle.
