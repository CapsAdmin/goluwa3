# Characters: pose, skeleton and attachments

Players and npcs share one character: `character_movement` consumes a `usercmd` from a controller (`player_controller`, `ai_controller`; `character_movement.RegisterController` adds more, `character_movement:GetCommand()` is the current command). Looks follow the same split.

```
usercmd -> character_movement (body yaw, velocity, grounded, crouch)
        -> character_pose     (replicated aim, crouch, holding; derived pitch, crouch_alpha, speeds)
        -> a driver writes the biped armature
        -> attachment_points  (named sockets on the armature)
        -> weapons attach to a socket, never to a raw bone
```

- `character_pose` is state only. `ViewRotation`, `Crouching`, `Holding` and the counters `Shots` and `Hits` replicate; `pitch` (view relative to the body, frozen when dead), `crouch_alpha`, `forward_speed`, `right_speed`, `vertical_speed` and `grounded` are computed every frame. Where there is a movement it reads the command, the local player reads `player_input` for zero latency, remote players use relayed commands. It exists on every character, so drivers and `weapon_holder` never look at a controller. `weapon:Think` calls `pose:RegisterShot()` after a primary fire and `health` damage reaches `pose:OnTakeDamage`; every client notices the counters change and starts `recoil` or `flinch` (both decay to 0), so fire and hit reactions need no extra packets. A driver sets `pose.driver` so only one writes the armature.
- The biped (`lua/biped.lua`) is the in-engine skeleton (`armature` skeleton `biped`, ~20 bones). It only exists where something drives it (client side, rendering) and humanoids only; creatures just animate their model.
- Drivers: `procedural_pose` (head and hands from the pose, the default outfit) and `source_pose` (plays Source sequences on the entity's `animator` from the pose, and with `Mirror` mirrors the animated Source bones into the biped through `biped.SourceBones`). `source_pose` has two profiles for sequence choice: `creature` (idle/walk/run sequence names, ground speed driven rate, `move_yaw`) and `gmod_player` (gmod player sequence naming by weapon, `move_x/move_y`). `OwnsArmature` makes the component add the armature to its own entity (npcs), otherwise the armature belongs to an ancestor (the player). It also sets the socket profile of its `attachment_points` (`SocketProfile`).
- Aim (`lua/aim_pose.lua`, pure functions over a table of bone matrices, tested without a rig): the pose pitch (plus recoil) is shared over spine, chest, neck and head (`aim_pose.Shares`, each joint rotates itself and everything above it about its own position, so the arms ride along); flinch leans spine and chest. While a weapon is held the right arm gets a two bone ik to a point `ArmReach` along the aim in front of the shoulder, and the armature hand takes the aim rotation (the grip socket and so the weapon point where the character aims). `source_pose` writes the aimed bones back into the animator rig as model space overrides so the mesh follows; it clears its own overrides before reading the bones each frame, otherwise the rotations would accumulate. `Quat:Lerp` mutates its receiver, use `GetLerped`.
- Sockets (`armature:SetSocket(name, bone, offset, rotation)`, listed by `GetSocketNames`) work like bones for `transform.Attachment`. `attachment_points` applies a named profile from `biped.SocketProfiles` (`grip_r`, `grip_l`, `eye`, `head`, `center`, `feet`) when its entity's armature is built (`OnArmatureRebuild`, `OnEntityConstructed` for prefab built entities, whose events are suppressed). A weapon model attaches to `grip_r`; per-model offsets or rotations go into a profile (`source_humanoid` moves the pistol grip into the animated fist).
- `weapon_holder` updates the model visibility of its weapons for players and npcs alike (hidden in first person through `pose.first_person`).
- Not done: left arm ik (two handed grips), a death pose, per-model profiles beyond `source_humanoid`, sockets for non-humanoid models (nothing needs one yet, they have no biped).
