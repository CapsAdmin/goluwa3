# JRPG mode

A port of the gmod jrpg addon, in the `jrpg` addon (`addons/jrpg/lua/jrpg/` plus its components in `addons/jrpg/lua/components/`), which depends on `game`. `!rpg` toggles it on the local player (`!rpg cheat` pins mana and stamina), `!level <attribute>` spends attribute points (no argument lists them), `!status <name> <amount>` applies a status to yourself.

- Being in rpg mode is having the `rpg` component (mana, stamina, xp, level, attributes; stats persist per player in `data/jrpg/`). `jrpg.SetEnabled(entity, on)` adds it, gives the loadout (sword in the right hand on right mouse, shield in the left raised with left mouse, one `weapon_magic_<type>` per damage type, potions) and puts the old max health, speeds and weapons back when removed. `RPGEnabled`/`RPGDisabled` events turn the battlecam on and off for the local player.
- Everything that is only presentation (jrpg HUD, damage numbers, npc bars, item highlights, damage flashes) checks `actors.IsLocalRPG()`; the plain HUD hides itself while it is on. Gameplay (statuses, projectiles, npc fights) runs regardless.

## Damage

`damage.Deal(victim, info)` -> `ModifyDamage` (jrpg: attack/defense attributes, heal sign, roll dodge, shield block, the type's `OnDamage`) -> `health:TakeDamage` -> `HealthDamaged` (effects, xp bookkeeping, knockback). `info.Type` is a damage type name (`jrpg/damage_types/*.lua`, listed in `damage_types.Names`), `info.Cancel = true` from a listener drops the hit. Damage dealt by a status sets `info.Status` so its type's `OnDamage` does not apply the status again.

- A damage type is a table: `Color`, `EndColor`, `Particles` (gpu emitter properties for bursts and projectiles), `Glow`, `Light`, `Flicker`, `Knockback`, `Heals`, `HitSounds`, `Adjectives`/`Names`, `OnDamage(victim, info)` (authority).
- Physical types (`damage_types.Physical`, `slash`, `Physical = true`) are registered like the others but are not in `damage_types.Names`, so they have no spell, and `IsMagic` is false for them (physical attack and shield rules). A sword hit (`slash.Strike` in `lua/jrpg/slash.lua`, shared by the sword and by `weapon_slash_debug`, a model-less, animation-less weapon in the rpg loadout whose clicks cycle down, up, left, right and stab at exact angles, print the cut and normal and draw the cut line (red), the normal (green), forward (blue) and the cut plane (cyan) for five seconds) deals damage at the cut of the weapon's type (`weapon:GetDamageType("slash")`: `slash` unless the weapon has an `Attribute`, see [weapons.md](weapons.md); an attributed hit is one hit of that type, not an extra one): every `ATTACKS` entry has a `cut` direction (right, up, forward of the character, the way the blade travels at the hit), the cut plane is the one that holds that direction and the character's forward, `Normal` is that plane's normal turned to the attacker's side (a thrust uses minus forward) and `Position` is where that plane meets the victim (the victim centre projected onto it, then a sweep from the attacker finds the surface and the hit part). `effects.Impact(position, normal, type, strength, cut, direction)` takes that cut direction (a Vec3, or nil for spells): with a cut the burst is a tight stream (2 degrees, `SLASH_SPREAD`) along the normal instead of the 55 degree cone and no ring. The sword also fires `effects.HitOverlay(victim, kind)` (a replicated event, `ShowHitOverlay` on the clients) for every victim `slash.Strike` returns: a self expiring surface overlay (`HIT_OVERLAY` in `effects.lua`: 0.35 s hold, 0.5 s fade) on each visual of the victim, `magic_generic` for now (`HIT_OVERLAY` in `weapons/sword.lua`). The cut sparks (`effects.BurstType` with `cut`) have no gravity, high drag so they stop a short way out, four times the damage type's lifetime and the emitter's `Fizzle`, which scatters them in noise directions in the last part of their life.
- Status effects (`jrpg/status_effects/*.lua`) live in the `status_effects` component: amounts 0..`Max` decaying by `Decay` per second, `Think` every `Rate` seconds on the authority, `Speed(amount)`, `CanAttack(amount)`, `AnimationSpeed(amount)`, `Particles`, `Overlay` (a `surface_overlays` kind from `lua/surface_overlays/status_*.lua`), `Orbit`. They replicate as one string (`fire=45 frozen=10`) that clients smooth. Change them only through `status_effects.Set/Add/Remove` on the authority.
- `PlayerMove` listeners may change `move.move`, `move.wish_speed` and `move.jump_pressed`; jrpg uses that for lunges, frozen, confused, blocking and stamina.
- A weapon or component returning false from `OnCanAttack` on the character blocks attacks (frozen, stunned, rolling, blocking).

## Effects and animation

- `jrpg/effects.lua`: functions called on the authority (`Impact`, `Damage`, `Text`, `Block`, `LevelUp`) are `replicated_event`s (`goluwa/network/replicated_event.lua`): broadcast on a server, run directly in a standalone game. Visuals exist only when the renderer is up (`effects.Visual`); headless tests get the routing alone. The look is compositions of the engine layer in [vfx.md](vfx.md) (`effects.vfx` is the module, only set when visual); weapons guard their visual calls with `effects.Visual`.
- Additive glows go through sprite batches, so a pixel's brightness is tint x texture and alpha is passed squared where the old per object materials squared it (`b.flare:Add(..., alpha * alpha)`). The refractive lens is the exception: its batch is keyed by colour and cannot fade, so its radius scales with alpha.
- Materials are registered for good in the scene material list (`render3d/scene/materials.lua` is append only and DDGI reads every entry), so never create or remove materials per hit: effects share tinted materials and pool the rest. A gpu emitter that is `Visible` holds one of `MAX_EMITTERS` slots even when not `Playing`, so hide emitters that are not needed.
- The dropped weapon highlight (`components/item_highlight.lua`) shades the item itself with the `item_highlight` surface overlay kind (`lua/surface_overlays/item_highlight.lua`, one layer per child visual, added while the weapon is dropped and removed when held), plus ribbons, glow sprites and motes. See [surface-effects.md](surface-effects.md). `lua/examples/overlays_showcase.lua` (run it with `lua`, nothing imports it) shows every status kind and the item highlight on spheres, and drops a few real weapons.
- Status particles are one gpu emitter per (entity, status) under a small budget; status looks are surface overlay layers (one per visual of the entity, intensity set from the status amount with `handle:SetIntensity`, rebuilt when the children list changes); the damage flash and low health pulse still use the forward `effects.AddOverlay` rim/fill material.
- One-shot character animations are replicated pose actions (`character_pose:PlayAction(name, duration)` on the authority, definitions in `lua/animations/actions.lua` with yaw/pitch `AimKeys` for swings, casts, drink); the base `character_movement` roll (hold crouch while moving on the ground; the `roll` state replicates its kind) rotates the rendered body and the view (`character_pose`), creatures play `source_pose.AttackSequence` during `attack`. The sword's strikes (`sword_light_1..3`, `sword_heavy`, `sword_jump`, `addons/jrpg/lua/animations/sword.lua`) use `AimKeys`, `HandKeys` and `TorsoKeys` ([characters.md](characters.md)) with a windup, a held beat, an eased-in snap and a follow-through; the sword model is tilted in its prefab so the blade stands up out of the fist, and a strike's `hit`, `swing` (trail and sound window), `cancel` (when a queued input chains) and `lunge` are fractions of its duration in `ATTACKS`. `character_movement:Lunge(direction, distance, delay, duration)` (base movement, see [characters.md](characters.md)) steps the character forward inside the strike on a half-sine speed curve (the character coasts roughly 0.05-0.2 m past the commanded distance at higher speeds, so the long lunges are tuned short), the legs play the gait clips while it carries the body and the sword's `PelvisKeys` dip and turn the hips over them, and the sword shortens it when a target is already close. The other actions are still placeholders.
- Npcs fight with a gun (`weapon_holder`), in melee (`ai_controller.MeleeDamage`) or with magic (`MagicElement`).

## Testing

`luajit glw test entities/jrpg.lua`. In-game harnesses need gm_construct for the source models: load it with `engines.Load("gm_construct")`, wait for `static_world.GetActive()`, then `commands.RunLine("rpg")`. Simulate keys with `input.CallOnTable(input, "Key", key, press)` plus `event.Call("KeyInput", key, press)` and `player_input:SetKeyboardMovement(true)`.

- Weapon categories (label, color) are registered in `lua/weapon_categories.lua` (`Register`, `Get`); a weapon only names its `Category`, and an unregistered name falls back to `unknown`. The game addon registers its own, jrpg adds `melee`, `magic` and `defense` from an autorun. The weapon ring colors its wedges from there and separates categories with a glow line.
- A sword hit deals its damage with `Reaction = <the attack's damage scale>` and `Direction = <the cut direction>`, so the victim plays the procedural `hit_react` (the pelvis shoved along the cut, stronger for harder attacks) instead of the plain flinch (see [characters.md](characters.md)).

## Weapon rarity and statuses

Ported from the gmod jrpg `wepstats`. `lua/jrpg/weapon_stats.lua` has nine rarities (`broken` to `godly`: damage multiplier, bonus range, color, how many positive and negative statuses it may have) and a status registry: every damage type except generic is a positive elemental status (the weapon's `Attributes`), plus `leech`, `fast`, `decay`, `deflect` (positive) and `clumsy`, `dull`, `dumb` (negative, in `Statuses`). A status has `Chance`, `Names`/`Adjectives`, an optional `Allowed(rarity)` and `Roll(weapon, rarity)` (applied once, like `fast`'s rate), and runtime hooks `OnDamage(weapon, attacker, victim, info)` (the inflictor weapon of a hit) and `OnReceiveDamage(weapon, victim, attacker, info)` (equipped weapons of the victim), called from `ModifyDamage` and skipped for `info.Status`, `info.Secondary` and `info.WeaponStatus` (set on damage the statuses deal themselves, so they do not trigger each other).

`Roll(weapon, rarity, bonus, names)` (nil arguments are random) picks the rarity with `random()^4`, a 25% chance of a bonus step count (each 2% strength, shown as `(+N)`), and statuses: positives with their chance up to the rarity's positive limit (three where the original was unlimited), negatives with `random() / tier < chance` up to the negative limit. The title follows the original: `<rarity> <negative adjectives and the first positive's adjective> <name> of <the other positives' names> (+N)`, e.g. `Epic Faded and Cold Sword of Embers and Quickness (+5)`. Rollable weapons (sword, pistol) are rolled when constructed, including the rpg loadout sword. The item highlight (beams, glow, motes, overlay and HUD label) uses the rarity color and rebuilds when the rarity changes.
