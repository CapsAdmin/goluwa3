# Scatter

Models (grass, flowers, rocks, bushes, trees) grown over surfaces on the gpu. Sets are property objects like materials (`goluwa/render3d/scatter_set.lua`; its header lists every key of a prototype's rules, unknown keys are errors); the gpu side is `goluwa/render3d/scatter.lua`; drawing and ray traced presence are the scene's draw streams (see [gpu-scene.md](gpu-scene.md)).

## How it fits together

- A material says what grows on it with `ScatterSet` (a set object) and `ScatterDensity`. `TerrainLayerScatter` is how much each of a terrain's first four layers grows; a prototype's `layers` picks from them. `ScatterSet.New{Name, Prototypes}` makes a set, `ScatterSet.AddFactory(name, create)` registers one made on first `ScatterSet.Get(name)` (the foliage sets are registered this way) and `ScatterSet.GetNames()` lists both kinds, `set:Use()` loads its models and gives each prototype a global index, `set:Remove()` takes it out. `DensityMultiplier` and `DistanceMultiplier` scale a set live (per prototype through `density_response` and `distance_response`). `ScatterSet.GetGrass()` is a builtin set needing no assets. Presets: `addons/game/lua/autorun/render_3d/scatter_presets.lua` (Crysis classes, empty without Crysis installed) and `addons/foliage/lua/foliage/scatter_sets.lua` (procedural, see [foliage.md](foliage.md)).
- `scatter.lua` only places instances; drawing them is the scene draw path's job (draw streams, see [gpu-scene.md](gpu-scene.md)). **Baking**: what a surface grows is baked in *units* (cells of a surface: 64/256/1024 m, split into 16 while the camera is within `BAKE_SPLIT` times the cell size) and kept. `PreDraw` bakes `BAKE_BUDGET` units a frame, nearest by `view_priority.lua` (the ranking terrain tiles also use), with two compute passes: `scatter_tiles` (jobs per triangle and prototype) and `scatter_instances` (hash, jitter, thin, test against triangle/layer mask/slope/altitude/cluster noise/a cover ray query; survivors are written as matrices into the unit's room in the `baked` pool). A unit is baked for a distance band and re-baked when the camera enters a nearer band or when the set, multipliers, surface or density change (`bake_epoch`).
- **Per frame**: `scatter_cull` (on `PreSceneBuild`) thins instances by exact distance, widens them, picks the model, mid or billboard tier and appends each to that tier's draw stream (`scene_draw.CreateStream`, one per prototype tier, its parts are the tier's meshes): the view list with the dither fade in `[0][3]` (both tiers during a band blend), and for `shadow` prototypes the shadow list, which is not view culled. The scene cull then frustum and size culls the view lists into the groups of the parts, so scattered models are routed to the visibility buffer by density, sided by `GetRasterBothFaces`, shaded with their own variant bits and drawn with the scene rows; the shadow cascades cull the shadow lists the same way.
- Placement is deterministic: a cell's hash decides jitter, priority and all randomness, and priority against the density at the camera's distance decides if an instance exists, so nothing moves or flickers as the camera does.
- The baked matrix's bottom row is data: `[0][3]` the density need (the dither fade once appended), `[1][3]` unused (0), `[2][3]` the prototype, `[3][3]` the conform corner (below). The prototype `color` and `tint` keys are parsed but nothing reads them.
- **Billboards**: `billboard = {distance, fade}` (or `{radii = n}` for placed) asks `render3d/billboard.lua` for a baked billboard of the model. Each prototype has a model and a billboard stream, dither-crossfaded around the distance. Shadow lists keep the model up to the billboard distance and the billboard beyond it. `r_scatter_billboards` and `r_scatter_billboard_distance` (scales the whole billboard zone and its reach, re-baking) are runtime pvars.
- **Mesh LOD**: `lod = {distance, fade}` on a prototype gives it a mid tier between the full model and its billboard, drawn from the model's coarsest mesh LOD polygons (`LODLevel > 0`; `scatter_set.lua` `finish_loading` collects them). The tier is a hidden companion prototype (`proto.mid`, `parent`, appended after every set's prototypes in `ScatterSet.Rebuild` so set ranges stay contiguous) that is never baked: the parent's cull appends into the companion's model stream, so rooms, shadow lists, demand and the ray traced sources treat it like any prototype. The switch is `lod.distance * distance_multiplier * billboard scale` (clamped under half the billboard switch, `get_mid_switch`) with the same dither crossfade band as the billboard. `r_scatter_lod` turns it off. A model without LOD polygons logs it and keeps the full model.
- **Slopes and ground**: `align` stands a model on the ground normal; `align_steep` is the share left on steep slopes. `conform = true` measures the ground under the footprint at bake time with ray queries and packs four edge heights plus a radius into `[3][3]`; `get_instance_conform_offset` in `deformation.lua` (a step of `deform()`) bends the vertices so the base meets the ground. It needs ray queries and the ground in the scene store; normals and the ray traced copy are not conformed.
- **Ray traced scene**: streams of prototypes with `shadow` and `bvh` are traced: the scene writes a produced row for each instance in their shadow lists (`r_rt_stream_distance`, `r_rt_streams` off). `bvh = false` / `shadow = false` on a prototype keep it out of rays / shadow maps. The conform word does not reach rays (deformed copies take the instance's 3x4 matrix).
- **Rooms**: each stream has a view and a shadow room, estimated at first and then following what the gpu counted (`scene_draw.GetStreamDemand`, which keeps counting past the room; rooms grow 1.5x or shrink). `max_instances` fixes the model view room. Baked units get rooms from `buddy_pool.lua` (power-of-two classes, buddies merge); first bakes of large cells with big estimates go in a small `PROBE_ROOM` and re-bake to fit. `scatter.GetPoolStatus()` shows where the pool went; look at it before believing it is full.

## Placed instances

Prototypes with `placed = true` have no placement rules; they are instances that stand where they were put (a level's painted vegetation) and go through the same cull, billboards, shadow lists, conform and ray traced scene. `scatter.AddPlacements(proto, matrices, count)` takes float[16] matrices as the cull pass reads them and returns a handle for `RemovePlacements` / `SetPlacementsHidden`. Instances are cut into `PLACED_CELL` units kept on the host and uploaded when the camera is within the prototype's reach. The `scatter_placements` component (`goluwa/entities/components/scatter_placements.lua`) is the way to use it; `crysis_loader/scene.lua` makes one per vegetation prototype. Its `Records` (packed `x, y, z, scale, yaw, nx, ny, nz` floats) are a hidden property and any property change places the instances again at the end of the frame; the editor's scatter brush (`terrain_editor.lua`) paints and erases records on whatever surface is under the cursor (visuals and terrains).

## Pitfalls

- Models need an index buffer (the multi draw pulls vertices through the index pool); LOD 0 polygons are the full model and the coarsest mesh level is the optional mid tier, other levels are ignored. Meshes from `shapes.Build*` must be uploaded with indices.
- `bending` sets `Bending` on the model's materials, which are shared with every other use of that model.
- A prototype with `shadow = true` is not cut by the camera frustum in shadow lists; shadow maps, recorded before the frame's cull, draw what the last frame appended.
- Thinning, tiers and tile jobs come from the main camera; a second view sees the main camera's instances.
- Past `MAX_JOBS` jobs are silently dropped: if a big scene loses chunks, check the job count. Sparse prototypes (cell above 2 m) only get jobs for triangles that contain a cell point.
- The tile pass box must be grown by the instance's radius, or crowns of trees whose root is out of view are lost. The cover ray is only used on surfaces without a layer mask (overlapping LOD tiles would hit each other).
- A surface must be in `Visual.Library.GetSceneMaterialUsers()` to grow anything; if a surface has no scatter, compare that with the visuals using the material before reading shaders.
- Dither fade comes only from distance thinning; fading by density turns bushes into ghosts. Prefer a cluster `amount` over a low `density` for patches.
- Terrain tiles hand over to replacements only once the replacements' vegetation is baked (`scatter.Preload`, `IsSurfaceReady`, `Terrain:IsTileReplaced` with `ScatterWaitFrames`); `SetRetiring` deprioritizes tiles about to go. Bake classes rank new units before re-bakes for a different band.
- Baking is cheap; drawing is the cost (triangle volume: a far tree of 10k triangles covers a few pixels, a view over a forest easily submits 10 triangles per pixel), so reduce instances (density/distance per class), use billboards earlier, or give the prototype a `lod`. Count what a view submits with `scene_draw.ReadBackCull(0)` (`triangles`, `streamed`, `by_group`) before guessing: fragment cost, vertex animation registers and overdraw (an alpha tested depth pre-pass and occlusion culling both measured as no gain) were not the limit.
- Tests need unique set names and mesh sizes (meshes with identical vertices are shared and may be on their way out).

- Data the gpu passes read that the cpu rewrites each frame (the prototype buffer) is recorded into the command buffer with `cmd:UpdateBuffer` (`upload`), never written to a host visible buffer: a frame still running reads the next frame's bases and rooms with the previous frame's buffers and indexes past their end.
- Ray traced rows are shown only in the frame they were written in, so the cull runs before the scene build (`PreSceneBuild`) and calls `scene_draw.EndStreams` after appending.
- Profile a flight before blaming the gpu: compare `gpu_frame` with the frame time (a CPU profile with `PROF` found the ray material table rewritten per consumer, scatter's unit visit and terrain tile heights). `island.Fly` follows the ground, so its altitude changes bake the atmosphere LUT; hold a constant altitude when measuring scatter.

## Verifying

`test/tests/render3d/scatter.lua` (red cubes on a plane, slope rules). Examples under `addons/examples/lua/examples/render3d/`: `scatter_showcase.lua`, `island_terrain.lua` (large scale; `island_view N`), `foliage_world.lua`. Wait for `scatter.pending_bakes` to reach 0 before measuring; after a camera jump baking takes seconds. `glw bench scatter_meadow` with `SCATTER_OFF=1` is the baseline.
