# Scatter

Models (grass, flowers, rocks, bushes, trees) grown over surfaces on the gpu. Sets are property objects like materials (`goluwa/render3d/scatter_set.lua`; its header lists every key of a prototype's rules, unknown keys are errors); the gpu side is `goluwa/render3d/scatter.lua`, ray traced presence is `scene/scatter_rt.lua` (see [gpu-scene.md](gpu-scene.md)).

## How it fits together

- A material says what grows on it with `ScatterSet` (a set object) and `ScatterDensity`. `TerrainLayerScatter` is how much each of a terrain's first four layers grows; a prototype's `layers` picks from them. `ScatterSet.New{Name, Prototypes}` makes a set, `set:Use()` loads its models and gives each prototype a global index, `set:Remove()` takes it out. `DensityMultiplier` and `DistanceMultiplier` scale a set live (per prototype through `density_response` and `distance_response`). `ScatterSet.GetGrass()` is a builtin set needing no assets. Presets: `addons/game/lua/autorun/render_3d/scatter_presets.lua` (Crysis classes, empty without Crysis installed) and `addons/foliage/lua/foliage/scatter_sets.lua` (procedural, see [foliage.md](foliage.md)).
- `scatter.lua` is a gbuffer extra (`gbuffer_multi_draw`, no pipeline of its own). **Baking**: what a surface grows is baked in *units* (cells of a surface: 64/256/1024 m, split into 16 while the camera is within `BAKE_SPLIT` times the cell size) and kept. `PreDraw` bakes `BAKE_BUDGET` units a frame, nearest by `view_priority.lua` (the ranking terrain tiles also use), with two compute passes: `scatter_tiles` (jobs per triangle and prototype) and `scatter_instances` (hash, jitter, thin, test against triangle/layer mask/slope/altitude/cluster noise/a cover ray query; survivors are written as matrices into the unit's room in the `baked` pool). A unit is baked for a distance band and re-baked when the camera enters a nearer band or when the set, multipliers, surface or density change (`bake_epoch`).
- **Per frame**: `scatter_cull` thins instances by exact distance, culls by view and writes the regions the draws read (model, billboard, and a separate shadow list that is not view culled); `scatter_commands` fills the indirect draws; `Draw` issues single and double sided indexed indirect draws with the PBR batch records of `gbuffer_instancing.NewBatchTable`, so scattered models shade exactly like scene models. Shadow maps draw `shadow_commands` through a `DrawAllShadows` listener.
- Placement is deterministic: a cell's hash decides jitter, priority and all randomness, and priority against the density at the camera's distance decides if an instance exists, so nothing moves or flickers as the camera does.
- The instance matrix's bottom row is data: `[0][3]` the density need (or dither fade in the draw), `[1][3]` tint (unorm8 rgb as float bits, `in_instance_tint` multiplies albedo), `[2][3]` the prototype, `[3][3]` the conform corner (below).
- **Billboards**: `billboard = {distance, fade}` (or `{radii = n}` for placed) asks `render3d/billboard.lua` for a baked billboard of the model. Each prototype has model and billboard regions, dither-crossfaded around the distance. Shadow lists keep the model up to the billboard distance and the billboard beyond it. `r_scatter_billboards` and `r_scatter_billboard_distance` (scales the whole billboard zone and its reach, re-baking) are runtime pvars.
- **Slopes and ground**: `align` stands a model on the ground normal; `align_steep` is the share left on steep slopes. `conform = true` measures the ground under the footprint at bake time with ray queries and packs four edge heights plus a radius into `[3][3]`; `get_instance_conform_offset` in `model_pipeline.lua` bends the vertices so the base meets the ground. It needs ray queries and the ground in the scene store; normals and the ray traced copy are not conformed.
- **Ray traced scene**: shadow lists become scene store instances every frame (`scatter_rt.lua`, hidden past `r_scatter_rt_distance`, `r_scatter_rt` off). `bvh = false` / `shadow = false` on a prototype keep it out of rays / shadow maps.
- **Rooms**: each prototype has a room in the instance buffer, estimated at first and then following what the gpu counted (the counter keeps counting past the room; counters are read back each frame, rooms grow 1.5x or shrink). `max_instances` fixes it. Baked units get rooms from `buddy_pool.lua` (power-of-two classes, buddies merge); first bakes of large cells with big estimates go in a small `PROBE_ROOM` and re-bake to fit. `scatter.GetPoolStatus()` shows where the pool went; look at it before believing it is full.

## Placed instances

Prototypes with `placed = true` have no placement rules; they are instances that stand where they were put (a level's painted vegetation) and go through the same cull, billboards, shadow lists, conform and ray traced scene. `scatter.AddPlacements(proto, matrices, count)` takes float[16] matrices as the cull pass reads them and returns a handle for `RemovePlacements` / `SetPlacementsHidden`. Instances are cut into `PLACED_CELL` units kept on the host and uploaded when the camera is within the prototype's reach. The `scatter_placements` component (`goluwa/entities/components/scatter_placements.lua`) is the way to use it; `cry_engine/scene.lua` makes one per vegetation prototype.

## Pitfalls

- Models need an index buffer (the multi draw pulls vertices through the index pool); only LOD 0 polygons are used. Meshes from `shapes.Build*` must be uploaded with indices.
- `bending` sets `Bending` on the model's materials, which are shared with every other use of that model.
- A prototype with `shadow = true` is not cut by the camera frustum in shadow lists; shadow maps draw what the last `PreDraw` wrote.
- The frustum test and tile jobs come from the main camera; a second view sees the main camera's instances.
- Past `MAX_JOBS` jobs are silently dropped: if a big scene loses chunks, check the job count. Sparse prototypes (cell above 2 m) only get jobs for triangles that contain a cell point.
- The tile pass box must be grown by the instance's radius, or crowns of trees whose root is out of view are lost. The cover ray is only used on surfaces without a layer mask (overlapping LOD tiles would hit each other).
- A surface must be in `Visual.Library.GetSceneMaterialUsers()` to grow anything; if a surface has no scatter, compare that with the visuals using the material before reading shaders.
- Dither fade comes only from distance thinning; fading by density turns bushes into ghosts. Prefer a cluster `amount` over a low `density` for patches.
- Terrain tiles hand over to replacements only once the replacements' vegetation is baked (`scatter.Preload`, `IsSurfaceReady`, `Terrain:IsTileReplaced` with `ScatterWaitFrames`); `SetRetiring` deprioritizes tiles about to go. Bake classes rank new units before re-bakes for a different band.
- Baking is cheap; drawing is the cost (vertex bound), so reduce instances (density/distance per class), use billboards earlier, or LODs.
- Tests need unique set names and mesh sizes (meshes with identical vertices are shared and may be on their way out).

## Verifying

`test/tests/render3d/scatter.lua` (red cubes on a plane, slope rules). Examples under `addons/examples/lua/examples/render3d/`: `scatter_showcase.lua`, `island_terrain.lua` (large scale; `island_view N`), `foliage_world.lua`. Wait for `scatter.pending_bakes` to reach 0 before measuring; after a camera jump baking takes seconds. `glw bench scatter_meadow` with `SCATTER_OFF=1` is the baseline.
