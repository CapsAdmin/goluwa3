# Navigation and NPCs

Source `.nav` meshes, pathfinding over them, and NPC prefabs that walk around a map with the animations of their Source model. Dated numbers are snapshots; re-measure before relying on them.

## Pieces

- `goluwa/codecs/nav.lua`: Source `.nav` decoder and encoder (versions 5 to 16, gm_construct is 16). `nav.Decode(str)` returns plain tables (`areas` with `id, flags, nw, se, ne_z, sw_z, connections[0..3], hiding_spots, place, ladders`, `ladders`, `trailing_bytes`), in Source units and axes. Directions are north (-y), east (+x), south (+y), west (-x). Visibility lists (most of the 7 MB of gm_construct.nav), encounter paths and approach areas are skipped, not kept. `nav.Encode` writes the current version without them, the tests use it.
- `goluwa/navigation/navmesh.lua`: generic mesh of convex areas seen from above (engine space, y up) with one way links, `navmesh.New()`, `AddArea{Polygon, Id, Flags, Cost, Blocked}`, `Connect(from, to, a, b)` (a, b are the ends of the shared edge), `Build`, `GetAreaAt(position, tolerance)` (the area under a point whose surface is closest in height), `GetNearestArea`, `GetHeight`, `GetRandomPoint(around, radius, allowed)`, `GetReachableAreas(from, options)` and `FindPath(from, to, options)`.
- `goluwa/source_engine/nav_mesh.lua`: `nav_mesh.FromData(decoded)` / `nav_mesh.Load(path)` turns the Source areas into a navmesh (corners to engine space, the shared edge of each connection from the overlap of the two rectangles in its direction, `CROUCH`/`JUMP` areas blocked, `AVOID` areas cost 5).
- `goluwa/entities/components/nav_mesh.lua`: the mesh of the world, `Path` is the `.nav` file, `nav_mesh.GetMesh()` the active one (`SetMesh(mesh)` for one made in code). `map_scene.Translate` puts it on the map root when `maps/<name>.nav` exists. Console `nav_show` draws the areas near the camera (green, orange for avoid, red for blocked).
- `addons/game/lua/components/npc.lua`: the brain, `locomotion.lua`: animation from movement, `addons/game/lua/npcs.lua`: the prefabs (`npcs.Definitions`, `npcs.Register`, `npcs.Spawn(name, position, yaw)`, `npcs.SpawnOnMesh(name)`), `autorun/npcs.lua` registers the components. Example `addons/examples/lua/examples/render3d/npc_walkers.lua`, harness `test/harnesses/npc/run.lua`.
- `clip:GetGroundSpeed(params)` on Source clips (meters per second, from the movement block of each animation, blended like the pose), 0 for clips without motion. Tests: `luajit glw test navigation/navmesh.lua source_engine/nav.lua entities/npc.lua` (one file per call).

## Pathfinding

- `FindPath` options: `Radius` (insets the ends of every portal so the path keeps that far from corners), `MaxClimb`/`MaxDrop` (meters of height per connection, a link records its `rise`), `Blocked` (a table whose keys are areas that may not be entered and links that may not be used), `SnapDistance` (how far off the mesh the start and goal may be, they are moved onto it). Returns nil and a reason, or `{points, areas, links, length}`.
- A* runs over areas, the cost of a connection is the distance between the portal midpoints it is entered by (times the `Cost` of the area), then the funnel algorithm pulls the string tight through the portals. Left and right of a portal are the ones of a frame with x right and z up, the opposite of what looking down at the engine's x/z feels like, getting that wrong sent paths through the far end of every portal. 0.26 ms per query on gm_construct (2271 areas, 11446 links).
- gm_construct.nav has islands and connections that need a jump or a long drop: 52 of 300 random pairs have no path with `MaxClimb = 0.45`, `MaxDrop = 3`. Pick destinations from `GetReachableAreas` (a flood fill with the same limits), not from the whole mesh.
- 636 of its areas are under the water pit at x -80, z 12 (up to 12 m deep). `nav_mesh` blocks areas that are under more than 0.6 m of a water volume or the ocean (`BlockFlooded`, run again when the number of volumes changes, the volumes are made after the mesh).

## NPCs

An npc prefab is a model, an `animator`, a `locomotion` and an `npc` on the root, `Entity.New{prefab = {Path = "npc_zombie"}}` (or `npcs.Spawn`). Inputs: `Wander`, `WanderRadius`, `Running`, `WalkSpeed`, `RunSpeed`. The console command `npc_spawn <name>[,count]` puts them on the mesh around the camera. `npcs.Definitions` has citizen, barney, combine_soldier, zombie, vortigaunt, antlion and headcrab, a new one is a table with the model, the idle, walk and run sequence names and the walk and run speed in Source units per second (what `GetGroundSpeed` says, `entities/npc.lua` checks them within 3 percent, a sequence that is used for walking and running, like the zombie's, is fine).

- `npc` has no physics of its own. It adds a `player_movement` in `walk` mode (the capsule, steps, slopes and friction of a player) and is its command source (`player_movement:GetCommandSource()` is `player_controller` or `npc`): `NextCommand(dt)` returns a `usercmd` once per physics tick, so walking to a point is a yaw and `forward`/`side` axes like keys. It only does that where `network.HasAuthority()`, elsewhere the transform is replicated (tested with a server and a headless client, the client sees `authority false` and a kinematic body).
- `MoveTo(position, running)`, `Stop()`, `Teleport(position, yaw)` (use this to place one, setting the transform does not move a dynamic body), `GetGoal/GetPath/IsMoving`. Local events `OnNPCArrived` and `OnNPCPathFailed`. `Wander` picks a point near it from the reachable areas after a random delay.
- Path following: waypoints are reached within 0.5 m (the goal 0.3 m), it turns at `TurnSpeed` and turns on the spot first above 100 degrees. Not moving 0.2 m in a second is stuck: the connection it was going through and the area ahead of it are blocked for 10 s and the path is found again, after 3 of those the goal is given up (`OnNPCPathFailed`). Not above the mesh (2 m of height) for 5 s puts it back on the closest point of the mesh (pushed off a ledge, thrown by the physgun).
- `player_movement` friction removes `StopSpeed * Friction * dt` of speed per tick and acceleration adds `Acceleration * dt * wish speed`, so a character slower than about 1.1 m/s never got going (the zombie and vortigaunt stood still with forward at 1). The npc sets `StopSpeed` to half its walk speed.
- Cost, and why far npcs have no body: a dynamic capsule on the gm_construct brush world costs about 0.4 ms per physics tick (0.28 ms of it in the `support` section of the step, the sweeps of `player_movement` the rest), 40 of them took the physics step to 17 ms and a frame to 130-240 ms (the 8 steps per frame cap makes it worse, slow frames run more steps). Now only the nearest `npc.MaxSimulated` (12) npcs that are within `PhysicsDistance` (15 m, 8 m more before one that has a body loses it) of the camera or a player keep a body. The others are in `fly` mode (collision off, no gravity, `NextCommand` moves them with `MoveBodyBy` over the height of the mesh within `MaxDrop`) and cost almost nothing, 40 npcs 23 to 31-36 ms frame, 5.6 ms physics step. Ranked every 4th tick. Without a camera or player (a dedicated server with nobody on) everything is simulated.
- `locomotion` reads `transform:GetRenderVelocity()` so it works for replicated npcs too: idle under 0.12 m/s (starts at 0.3), walk and run switch at the middle of the two ground speeds, `animator:SetSpeed(speed / ground speed)` so feet do not slide, `move_yaw` is the angle of the velocity against the facing (positive is left, blends of -180 to 180).

## Things found on the way

- `Entity.New{...}` initializes every component of the entity a second time at the end of the construct (also the ones a prefab added on the way). `Visual:Initialize` reset `LoadGeneration` and so threw away the load of a model that a root `model` component of a prefab had started (`Entity.New{model = {ModelPath = "x.mdl"}}` never loaded, "visual became invalid while loading"). `visual`, `npc` and `locomotion` skip the second Initialize, anything new that creates things in Initialize and sits on a prefab root has to as well.
- `vmt.Load` never applied the blocks of a VMT named like `">=dx90_20b"` or `"<dx90"` (the base texture of every combine soldier lives inside one, they were pure white). They are applied now as on a modern card (`apply_dx_blocks`, level 95: `>=` blocks are used, `<` blocks are not).
- `test/tests/source_engine/vmt_material.lua` does not load by itself (import loop vmt_material -> scatter_set -> model_loader -> mdl -> vmt_material), not caused by this work.
- Spawn order matters little, but `Entity.New{prefab = ..., transform = {Position = p}}` builds the prefab before the position is applied and a dynamic body does not follow a transform, `npcs.Spawn` calls `Teleport` for that.
- Harness: `luajit glw --3d --background --validate lua "import('test/harnesses/npc/run.lua')"` (env `NPC_COUNT`, `NPC_SECONDS`, `NPC_SHOT=path.png`, `NPC_FOLLOW=<index>`, `NPC_DEBUG=1` draws the mesh and the paths, `NPC_QUIET=1`), it prints arrivals, failed paths, npcs off the mesh and the average frame (leave out `--validate` for that number). `net_server.lua` and `net_client.lua` next to it replicate an npc to a headless client.

Not done: ladders and jump connections of the nav file, crouching through crouch areas, doors and dynamic obstacles in the mesh, attack/idle/turn animations (Source turn sequences, gestures), activities (sequences are chosen by name per prefab, there is no activity table), npcs without a `.nav` (a mesh made from the world geometry).
