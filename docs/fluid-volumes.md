# Fluid volumes

Simulated or procedural gas in a box: smoke, fire, poison, steam, fog banks. Sources: `goluwa/render3d/fluid_volumes.lua` (registry, grid state, simulation kernels, light bake, atlas, records, shared GLSL), `fluid_volume_passes.lua` (the simulate pass, run as `passes/fluid_volumes.lua` before `lighting` so the lighting reads this frame's atlas, and the raymarch view pass, spliced into `passes/volumetric_fog.lua` before the composite), components `fluid_volume` and `fluid_emitter`. Example: `addons/examples/lua/examples/render3d/fluid_scene.lua` (`CAM=fire|poison|steam|overview`, `fluid_scene.Puff()`, `fluid_scene.Drop()` a crate into the poison, `fluid_scene.Roll()` a ball, `fluid_scene.Explode(pos)` and `fluid_scene.Barrage()` spawn pooled explosions, a burning cart drives back and forth with a world space volume; it sets no sun, it has a shadowed street lamp, a shadowed lamp over the poison pit, a spot through the steam and a lamp in the fog bank, for night). Tests: `test/tests/render3d/fluid_volumes.lua`.

## Model

- A volume is a uniform grid over its `Size` (the box is snapped up to whole cells; `Resolution` cells along the longest side, at most 128), in the entity's local frame. Fields per cell: velocity (m/s) and four scalars `(smoke, temperature in kelvin, fuel, flame)`. `smoke` is the concentration of the volume's `Medium` (a `media.presets` name; coefficients times `Density`), `flame` is luminous soot with the `flame` preset's absorption.
- `Simulation = "fluid"` steps a stable fluids solver per frame (up to `MAX_SUBSTEPS` of `MAX_STEP`): semi-Lagrangian velocity advection with Boussinesq buoyancy (`Buoyancy` times `(T - T_ambient) / T_ambient`), `Weight` (gas heavier than air sinks), turbulence noise forcing, emitters, vorticity confinement, then a red-black SOR pressure solve and projection. Box faces are open (inflow is the wind, pressure 0); `Floor` makes the bottom layer solid.
- Smoke uses MacCormack advection with the revert-to-semi-Lagrangian limiter; temperature, fuel and flame stay semi-Lagrangian. Sharpened temperature makes one cell thick bright streaks, because emission goes like `exp(-22707 / T)`.
- Combustion: fuel above `IgnitionTemperature` burns at `BurnRate`, heats by `HeatRelease` per unit up to `FlameTemperature` (the adiabatic limit) and becomes flame, flame oxidizes over `FlameLife` into smoke (`SootYield`). Temperature relaxes to ambient at `Cooling`. Every absorber emits `absorption * blackbody(T)` (Kirchhoff), using `blackbody_luminance` in `glsl_common.GetBlackbodyGLSL`, so hot smoke glows and cool gas does not.
- `Simulation = "procedural"` refills the grid every frame from noise scrolled by the wind (`Fill`, `NoiseScale`, `NoiseAmount`): no solver cost, for fog banks and other large, slow media.
- `Obstacles` voxelizes the scene into the grid with ray queries every `OBSTACLE_INTERVAL` seconds (cells whose short axis rays hit something are solid). It waits until the scene has built instances; without ray queries only `Floor` exists.
- Moving bodies (`Pushers`, fluid simulation only): every frame the non-static rigid bodies overlapping a volume (the bodies vegetation bends for: props, characters; at most `MAX_PUSHERS` each) are rasterized by a pushers kernel into the solid mask, as rounded oriented boxes (spheres and capsules exact) with their velocity plus spin. Inside cells move with the body and lose their gas, cells within `PUSHER_REACH` cells are dragged toward it at `PUSHER_DRAG`, and divergence and projection treat the solid as a moving boundary, so the pressure solve shoves gas aside ahead of a body and pulls a wake behind it. Velocity comes from each body's interpolated position history (kinematic character bodies have no solver velocity), jumps above `TELEPORT_SPEED` read as zero. Gas swept into a body faster than the pressure push is deleted (about a tenth of a layer a body rises through in the tests).
- Emitters (`fluid_emitter`: `Radius`, `Smoke` and `Fuel` per second at the center, `Temperature`, `Velocity` in the emitter's frame) and splats (`fluid_volumes.Splat(pos, radius, {smoke, temperature, fuel, velocity, expand})`, a one step impulse; `expand` pushes outward in m/s) each feed one volume: the finest (smallest cell) that contains their center, so overlapping volumes do not emit twice. Splats wait for a frame that steps (the first frame after a spawn has no step time).
- `Space = "world"`: the box is axis aligned and follows its entity in whole cell steps; the shift kernel moves velocity, scalars and static obstacles back by the same cells and clears the exposed slices, so the gas stays in the world and a moving emitter leaves a trail. A jump of a box size or more refills. Use it for fires attached to moving things and for spawned volumes; `"local"` (default) carries the gas rigidly with the entity (a cabin). Fast movers want particles or trails, not grids.
- Grid state is pooled: a removed volume's textures go to a cache (`MAX_FREE_STATES`) and the next volume with the same cell counts takes them, so spawning does not allocate. `fluid_volumes.Prewarm(size, resolution, count)` fills the cache and grows the atlas ahead of time.
- `fluid_volumes.Spawn{position, radius, smoke, temperature, fuel, velocity, expand, offset, volume = {component properties}}` splats into an existing transient world space volume that contains the event with `radius` to spare, or creates one (box at `position + offset`), taking the oldest when `MAX_TRANSIENTS` are alive. Transients are removed when the stats say they are spent (little smoke, no flame, ambient temperature) after `TRANSIENT_MIN_AGE`, or at `TRANSIENT_MAX_AGE`.

## Lighting and rendering

- After stepping, a light pass writes each volume into two shared 3d atlases (stacked along z, sampled with clamped texel coordinates so filtering never crosses volumes): the fields, and `(smoke optical depth to the sun, mean smoke optical depth over the upper hemisphere, shadow map sun visibility, flame optical depth to the sun)`. Optical depths are in concentration times meters so the spectral medium coefficients apply at render time; renderers only do lookups.
- `Raymarch = true` (small, near, detailed): `fluid_volumes_view` marches each pixel through the boxes (front to back, step `STEP_CELLS` cells, at most `MAX_STEPS`), with the sun through `medium_multiple_scattering`, ambient from the froxel GI grid and sky, local lights without shadows, and emission. It outputs pre-exposed scatter plus a transmittance weighted depth and rgb transmittance; the fog composite splits the froxel fog at that depth. Translucent surfaces and particles behind a raymarched volume are drawn over it.
- `Raymarch = false` (large, coarse): the froxel scatter and integrate passes sample the atlas like fog volumes, so it fogs translucents and particles too, at froxel resolution.
- Shadows: the bake marches the sun depth for solid cells too, so the light atlas holds the optical depth from any point in the box to where the sun ray leaves it. `fluid_sun_transmittance(world, sun_dir, skip_index, skip_mode)` intersects the sun ray with each box and reads that depth at the entry point: one fetch per volume, no march. It multiplies the sun on surfaces (`surface_lighting` under `#define FLUID_SHADOWS`, set by the lighting pass), the sun of the froxel air, ground fog and fog volumes (froxel mode fluids take only the raymarched ones, their own body is in their bake), and the sun of each raymarched volume from the others (once per volume along the view ray). Not yet: translucent surfaces, particles, water.
- Local lights: per volume the CPU picks up to `MAX_LIGHT_SLOTS` (4) point and spot lights in range, brightest over distance squared first (light indices into the shader lights array, so only the first `scene_lights.MAX_LIGHTS`), and the bake writes two more atlases: the smoke depth from each cell to each slot light (midpoint steps over the whole distance, whole cell steps make rings) and the light's medium visibility at the cell (point and local directional shadow maps, light occlusion; `medium_lighting.GetLightVisibilityGLSL`). The raymarch view multiplies each slot light by its transmittance and visibility; lights beyond the slots light the gas unshadowed. Surfaces read the slot depth at the box entry point of the segment to the light (`fluid_local_light_transmittance`), so smoke shadows lamps the same way it shadows the sun. Froxel mode fluids and the froxel air do not use the slots yet.
- Fire as a light (`EmitLight`): the bake sums the emitted luminous intensity (`absorption * blackbody luminance * cell volume`), its weighted center and temperature into the stats; a hidden `light_point` entity per volume follows them a few frames late (`4 pi I` lumens, blackbody color of the mean temperature, smoothed by `FIRE_LIGHT_RESPONSE`). It is an ordinary light, so it takes a slot of its own volume and its smoke shades it. Its brightness follows the flame physically: a 0.5 m flame near 1850 K emits about 1e5 lm, lower `FlameTemperature` for a dimmer wood fire.
- `fluid_volumes.GetStats(volume)` reads back per volume maxima, total smoke, the smoke weighted mean height and solid cell count (a few frames late). Use it in tests instead of pixels.

## Pitfalls

- Coarse grids have no subgrid entrainment: full strength Boussinesq buoyancy shoots one cell thin jets. Buoyancy defaults low and `Turbulence` stands in for the missing mixing.
- Fields are fp16; froxel mode clamps emission to stay finite, prefer raymarch for fire.
- Not yet: liquids (no free surface), fluid shadows on surfaces, fluid in DDGI or reflection rays, volumes that move keep their contents in local space.
